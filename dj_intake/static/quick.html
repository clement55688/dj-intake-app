DJ Intake

Your next track, one paste away.

How it works
  1. Paste a YouTube, SoundCloud or Bandcamp track link, a Spotify track link, or type Artist — Song.
  2. Click Add to queue. Song names and Spotify links need you to pick a recording in the full library.
  3. Hear a song you don’t know? Play it out loud and click Identify song — Shazam names it.
  4. Finished files are in Library in the full library, or your download folder (⚙).
